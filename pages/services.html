Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trials, destination travel, skin allergies, and booking deposits.

Most Lumina brides secure their wedding dates 6 to 12 months in advance, especially for peak November–February and May–June weekends. We recommend scheduling your 3-hour private trial 45 to 60 days prior to the wedding once your jewelry and neckline are finalized.

Micro-HD uses ultra-refined cream pigments hand-buffed with Japanese Kumano brushes for rich, dimensional radiance. Silicone Airbrush atomizes a micro-fine breathable mesh through a 0.25mm brass nozzle—ideal for outdoor humidity, tear resistance, and 18+ hour wear. You can test both side-by-side during your trial.
