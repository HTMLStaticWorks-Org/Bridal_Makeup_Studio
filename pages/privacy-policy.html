Privacy & Portrait Confidentiality

How Aurelle Bridal Studio safeguards your skin profile, trial photographs, and private venue details.

1. Bridal Dossier & Skin Data Collection

When you book a trial or ceremony suite with Aurelle Bridal Studio, we collect your name, contact details, wedding dates, venue address, skin type, undertone classification, and disclosed ingredient sensitivities solely to formulate your custom bridal service.

2. Private Trial Portraits & NDA Protection

All high-resolution photographs captured during your 3-hour private bridal trial are stored in your encrypted Bride Dashboard solely for face-chart reference. We never publish trial or wedding morning images to our public gallery or social channels without your explicit written release after your ceremony.

3. Escrow Payment & Retainer Security

All 25% retainer deposits and installment payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Stripe and PayPal). Aurelle Bridal Studio never stores raw credit card numbers on our servers.

4. Your Rights & Dossier Deletion

You may download, modify, or request permanent deletion of your bridal dossier and uploaded inspiration images at any time from your Bride Dashboard Settings or by emailing concierge@aurellebridal.studio.
