The Maharani Rose-Gold Velour Look

An interplay of antique champagne gold leaf eyelids, weightless silicone airbrush skin, and crushed mulberry-rose velvet lips.

The Look Story

Crafted for Candlelit Mandaps & Royal Courtyards

Commissioned originally for a sunset ceremony at Taj Lake Palace, the Maharani Rose-Gold Velour look bridges heritage regality with modern editorial skin. Rather than heavy matte powder that dulls under palace chandeliers, our artists layer a warm rose-quartz liquid illuminator beneath an 18-PSI silicone airbrush veil.

The eyes are framed with hand-pressed 24K champagne gold leaf across the mobile lid, anchored by smoked mulberry-espresso along the outer V and 52 individual knot-free cashmere lash flares.

Macro Angles

Close-Up Artistry Slider

Angle 01 / 03 — Eye Macro: Champagne gold-leaf pigment pressed over rose-bronze cream shadow with 8mm–13mm individual silk clusters.

Angle 02 / 03 — Cheekbone & Temple Profile: Seamless transition between micro-airbrushed skin, liquid rose-gold highlight, and kundan ear chains.

Angle 03 / 03 — Lip & Veil Harmony: Three-layer transfer-proof mulberry-rose velvet lip calibrated to zardozi embroidery.
