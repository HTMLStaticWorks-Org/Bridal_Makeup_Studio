The 90-Day Pre-Bridal Glass-Skin Preparation Ritual

By Aurelia Vance • Published November 12, 2026

1. 90 Days Out: Rebuilding Your Lipid & Ceramide Cushion

Whether you select our micro-atomized silicone airbrush or hand-buffed micro-HD cream foundation, your makeup can only sit as smoothly as your stratum corneum allows. Ninety days prior to your wedding vows is the window to repair micro-dehydration caused by travel and wedding-planning stress.

Introduce a 5-ceramide barrier emulsion every evening and pair it with low-molecular polyglutamic acid on damp skin. Unlike surface hyaluronic acid alone, polyglutamic peptides lock 4x more moisture inside the upper epidermis, creating the coveted lit-from-within bounce.

2. 45 Days Out: Your Bridal Trial & Active Exfoliant Cut-Off

Forty-five days prior to the ceremony is when you enter our flagship suite for your 3-hour bridal trial. Seven days before your trial—and twenty-one days before your wedding morning—we require all brides to pause prescription tretinoin, high-percentage AHA peels, and aggressive dermaplaning.

Why? Freshly stripped skin lacks the microscopic lipid grip needed for 18-hour foundation polymers, leading to patchiness around the nasal folds and chin.

3. 7 Days Out: Cryo-Lymphatic Drainage & Lip Peptide Wrapping

In the final week, shift exclusively to calming anti-inflammatory hydration. Store rose-quartz cryo-globes in your refrigerator and perform five minutes of upward lymphatic sweeps each morning. Sleep with a thick marine-collagen lip mask so velvet oxblood or rose-nude stains glide on without a single dry line.
