Our Origin Story

Where Editorial Precision Meets Sacred Tradition

Why founder Aurelia Vance created a private sanctuary where brides never feel rushed or masked.

The Flagship Sanctuary

Designed Like a Couture Dressing Salon

After a decade keying runway beauty in Paris and Mumbai, Aurelia noticed that brides were often treated to heavy theatrical paint under flattering salon bulbs that failed in daylight. She founded Lumina around calibrated 5500K daylight arches and skin-first airbrush chemistry.

Skin-First Philosophy

Every Lumina artist trains for 600 hours in undertone color theory, cryo-lymphatic drainage, and structural veil distribution. When you walk down the aisle, guests should marvel at your radiance—never the makeup itself.
