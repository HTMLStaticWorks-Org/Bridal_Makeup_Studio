Terms of Service & Booking Policy

Clear, transparent terms governing bridal retainers, rescheduling flexibility, and destination travel.

1. 25% Retainer & Calendar Exclusivity

A 25% retainer deposit is required to lock your wedding date and assigned lead artist on our calendar. Because we cap our weekend commissions at two brides per lead artist, dates are held strictly upon retainer confirmation.

2. Date Rescheduling & Trial Credit

Should your wedding muhurat or venue date shift, your retainer transfers seamlessly at zero fee when requested at least 45 days prior to your original ceremony date. Your $240 private trial fee is 100% credited when upgrading to The Aurelle Sovereign or Empress Destination Suite.

3. Final Balance & Destination Logistics

The remaining 75% suite balance is due 14 days prior to your ceremony date. For destination palace or villa weddings outside Los Angeles County, airfare and two nights of suite accommodation at the wedding property are arranged by the client concierge.

4. On-Time Arrival & Standby Artist Guarantee

Aurelle Bridal Studio guarantees arrival 20 minutes prior to your scheduled call time. In the rare event of a medical emergency, a co-lead creative director briefed on your exact face chart and pigment recipe is on standby.
